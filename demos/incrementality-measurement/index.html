Shared metric and execution definitions

  • Intention to treat: compare all assigned units, regardless of delivery or engagement.
  • Net revenue: deduct discounts and mature refunds consistently; do not deduct them twice.
  • Incremental contribution: difference in net revenue minus product/fulfillment costs and marketing costs.
  • iROAS: incremental net revenue / positive incremental marketing spend; distinct from ROI and marginal spend response.
  • 95% interval: coverage of a specified repeated procedure under assumptions; not a 95% probability that the claim is true.
  • Execution: recheck consent, eligibility, inventory, frequency, spend limits, idempotency and rollback before any real action.

Connectors: not implemented. Power analysis: not implemented. Geo, switchback and ITS analysis: planning only. Public case disclosures: cited, not re-estimated.
