# The Five Layers as a Decision and Learning Cycle

*[中文版 / Chinese version](five-layers-cognitive-cycle.zh.md)*

Reviewed: 2026-09-30. Five layers are a useful functional decomposition, not a universal industry taxonomy. One product, service or team can own several responsibilities. Separate responsibilities where this improves accountability; do not build five services merely to match the diagram.

The objective is to choose and deliver actions that create long-term incremental value under budget, consent, fulfillment and customer-experience constraints, then update decisions using evidence.

| Responsibility | Question | Output | Shorthand |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data | What was observed, inferred and recorded? | Events, identity links, consent, provenance | Records |
| Intelligence | What outcomes or intervention effects can we estimate? | Scores, embeddings, effect estimates, uncertainty | Beliefs |
| Decision | Which eligible action should we choose? | Action or no action, constraints and rationale | Choices |
| Activation | Can we deliver this authorized action correctly? | Delivery records, failures, realized exposure | Actions |
| Measurement | What does the evidence support, under which assumptions? | Estimates, intervals, diagnostics, learning | Evidence |

## 1. Data: records with provenance

Own event definitions, identity resolution, timestamps, consent and deletion state. Observed purchases, probabilistic identity matches and predicted propensity have different evidential status even when stored in one warehouse. Keep source, observation/inference type, method/version, confidence where meaningful, effective time and allowed uses. A probability is a model output, not proof that two identifiers are the same person.

Failures include duplicated conversions, timezone mismatch, missing exposure logs, delayed refunds and inconsistent consent. Reconcile orders and costs against business systems before blaming modeling.

## 2. Intelligence: estimates with a defined target

Prediction estimates outcomes under the data-generating policy; causal modeling estimates intervention contrasts under identification assumptions. High purchase probability does not imply high treatment effect. Record population, horizon, treatment definition and training policy.

RL spans Intelligence and Decision: learning a policy belongs here; applying it under live constraints belongs to Decision. Validate distribution shifts. Logs generated by selective targeting cannot automatically identify outcomes for untreated people or unobserved actions.

## 3. Decision: constrained choices

Choose audience, bid, offer, timing, channel or no contact. Start with a transparent rule baseline. Compare incremental contribution after discounts, returns, fulfillment and marketing costs; attributed revenue alone can reward unprofitable activity.

Historical average return is different from return on additional spend. Expansion requires local experimental evidence or a supported response curve, with a range of validity. Missing evidence can justify another experiment or a documented risk-limited business choice; it does not justify calling a proposal proven.

## 4. Activation: an action contract

Verify eligibility, consent, frequency caps, inventory, offer validity and spending limits at execution time. Specify permissions, idempotency keys, retries, cancellation, rollback and human escalation. Log proposed and approved actions, delivered exposure, failure reason and policy version. Delivery success and business success are separate outcomes.

Choosing and executing remain logically different when one platform performs both. A conversation can contain new decisions; it is not necessarily a passive channel.

## 5. Measurement: evidence and uncertainty

Experiments, observational causal methods, MMM and attribution provide different evidence. Attribution assigns credit under a rule/model; it does not by itself identify incrementality. MMM causal interpretation requires assumptions, informative variation, confounding control and sensitivity analysis. [Meridian's introduction](https://developers.google.com/meridian/docs/basics/meridian-introduction) explains posterior uncertainty and response curves.

Before running a confirmatory experiment, define estimand, assignment unit, observation window, outcome maturity, exclusions, business threshold and guardrails. Analyze at the assignment unit and account for interference, clustering and carryover. See [switchback design and analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.00148).

A fixed-window analysis must not stop when an ordinary p-value first crosses a threshold. Monitor operational safety separately from efficacy. Report point estimates, intervals, missingness and diagnostics. A p-value is not the probability that a hypothesis is true; `1 − p` is not a confidence score. Positive effects may still fail business requirements. See the [ASA statement](https://www.amstat.org/asa/files/pdfs/P-ValueStatement.pdf).

## Boundaries and feedback

```text
Data → Intelligence → Decision → Activation → Measurement
  ↑                                  │           │
  └──── observed outcomes + logs ─────┘           │
  └──────── experimental records ─────────────────┘
          Intelligence ← evidence and uncertainty
```

| Boundary | Engineering consequence |
|---|---|
| Records / inference | Preserve provenance and uncertainty in storage. |
| Prediction / choice | Combine scores with costs, causal relevance and constraints. |
| Choice / execution | Diagnose strategy and delivery failures separately. |
| Execution / evidence | Evaluate delivered actions against a counterfactual. |

Record eligibility, assignment probabilities and exposure so future analysts can understand selection. Exploration needs evaluation design and permissions. Repeatedly feeding selected outcomes into a model does not guarantee correct causal learning.

Platforms and governance support every responsibility. Agents and workflows orchestrate work, but orchestration does not supply a counterfactual. Systems can operate without causal measurement; their ability to claim incremental value is then limited.
